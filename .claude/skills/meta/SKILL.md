# Meta mode

**Target:** the subject passed with this skill. If none was passed, ask for it before acting.

Build or maintain the apparatus: **tooling** (agents, skills, hooks, scripts, the graph), **methodology**
(the rules, conventions, decisions), and **maintenance** (reconcile the board, repair provenance, fix
doc drift). It improves how we work; it touches no science number and flips no rung.

## The standard

The hard line: no science number is produced or consumed as a result, and no rung moves. If the work
starts needing a number, it has become `/work` or `/interpret`: re-enter that stance and state it.
Adaptive semistructure (`docs/03-methodology.md`): add a rule, agent, or artifact only when its absence
is costing truth or continuity, never preemptively; a workflow reconstructed twice earns a structure.

## Records

A real methodology decision goes in `docs/decisions/decisions.md`; a tooling change is committed
atomically with a conventional message (D007).

## Boundary

Changes the apparatus, not the science. It produces no evidence and adjudicates no result.
