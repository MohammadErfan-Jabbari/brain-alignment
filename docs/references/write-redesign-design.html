The one idea

Good academic writing is not one skill. It is the separation of four normally-tangled concerns: what you claim and whether it is evidence-bound · why it follows and whether it is honestly sized · how it is ordered for a reader · the voice it is said in. Tangle them and you get our failure: a draft that is true, but reads like a story, over-reaches its scope, and no one can tell which sentences to trust without re-reading every line.

Trust + Argument = the case is true, sound, and honestly sized. Structure + Voice = it is delivered well. The human gates the case and the plan — never the lines. Trust is then earned by a gate plus a high-recall audit, not asserted "by construction."

What · Trust

Claims ↔ evidence

Every claim enumerated and bound to a recorded result, tagged by evidence strength, before prose. Wording bounded by strength. Drift detected, not trusted.

Why · Argument

Soundness & honestyNEW

Claims connect by stated warrants into the central inference. Novelty and significance are themselves evidence-bound claims. Scope ≠ strength: an anti-over-claim gate (Opening-width = Resolution-width).

How · Structure

Reader-expectation

Skeleton fixed first, fractally (context→content→conclusion at every scale). Old-to-new at the sentence — relative to an explicit reader-model. Importance, not chronology.

Voice · Register

Scientific document

A document, not a story. No agency to abstractions, no narration of the argument, no AI tells, no unmotivated passive. The residual that is genuinely taste.

↳ narrative arc (logical problem→resolution) is Structure and is required; storytelling register (drama, agency to abstractions) is Voice and is banned. Different things — the design names the line so two auditors can't clash.

The pipeline — a gated loop, then a draft funnel

The front three stages co-determine each other and are gated by the human as one coupled package. Only then does prose get generated, audited, and revised.

The four dimensions run through every stage

The spine is the sequence; these are the quality axes checked continuously along it. A stage is not done until all four hold for its output.

Trust

Does every claim trace to recorded evidence, at the strength its wording implies? Born at stage 2, enforced through draft and audit.

Argument

Do the claims connect by valid warrants into the central inference — and is no claim scoped beyond its evidence? The half that makes a thesis a thesis, and keeps us honest. Born at stage 2, the hardest thing to audit (stage 5).

Structure

Does the reader meet old before new, context before content, at every scale — for this reader? Born at stage 1 (reader-model) + 3, realized in the draft.

Voice

Does it read as a scientific document — no story, no agency to abstractions, no tells? The residual taste; the floor (linter) plus a real reader.

The honesty gate (why this design is ours, not a paper mill)

The imported canon is a persuasion toolkit — it optimizes for landing a contribution and assumes you co-design the result with the writing. Our evidence is frozen and recorded. So the story-design machinery is subordinated to an explicit anti-over-claim rule: scope ≤ evidence, strength = evidence, Opening-width = Resolution-width. The system must be as good at writing "undemonstrated, not proven-zero" as at writing a win. That is the Argument/Honesty concern's job, and it has no analog in any repo we audited.

Why this earns trust (corrected: by gate + audit, not "by construction")

  • The case is locked and the human approves it — message, claims, warrants, scope, and skeleton, as one coupled package. Expensive judgment spent where it is cheap and decisive.
  • Voice and scope are first-class, continuously enforced — not a "small residual" cleaned up at the end. The ground-truth failure (Claudio) was born in generation; it gets a dedicated, high-recall reader, not an afterthought.
  • Prose is a projection of an approved case onto reader-expectation rules, then audited for drift — trustworthy to the degree the audit's recall is high. The honest claim, not the slogan.

The fluidity principle — methods are defaults, not laws

The methods named in each functionality (CCC / OCAR / CARS, the strength ladder, the detox list) are a starting toolkit, not a closed rulebook. At any functionality, if the listed methods don't fit the case in front of it, the agent should stop, diagnose why they don't fit, and then — adapt or combine them, find a better one, invent one, or flag you to co-think when the choice is load-bearing or it is unsure. A method that proves itself gets written back as a new option (the same way our reasoning toolkit grows).

The one boundary, so fluid never means fragile: this freedom lives in the how — the craft and method choices, the RUB layer. The trust & honesty floor stays rigid: a claim always needs bound evidence, scope never exceeds evidence, no prose ships before the gate, provenance tags are always emitted. And every deviation is logged with its reasoning — fluid, never silent. (The DET checks are exactly the rigid floor; the RUB ones are exactly where the agent may think for itself.)

Step 2 review · CC-power upgrades

A final clean-context opus review (CC-expertise lens) returned BUILD-READY and flagged where the mapping under-used Claude Code. Applied:

  • Parallel stage-5 audits — F4/F5(prose) · F9b · F11 · F12 · F13 · F19 are independent; fan out in one message, not sequentially (~4× wall-time, one context build).
  • Stop-hook convergence (F14) — the loop's "don't finish until clean" is a Stop-hook that re-blocks until every verdict is ready_to_ship (the D047 pattern, pipeline-scoped). The revise procedure stays a skill-section.
  • Structured-output verdicts — every auditor returns schema-forced JSON with a ready_to_ship: bool, so the Stop-hook reads a boolean, not prose. (Formalizes the panel's existing PANEL-VERDICT blocks.)
  • Gate via AskUserQuestion / ExitPlanMode (F16) — a real approval primitive, not ad-hoc text.

Effort (Erfan-approved): HIGH for all subagents; XHIGH for the 3 hardest judges — F4 argument, F5 scope, F11 structure. Optional / lower-leverage (not yet applied): lattice GET/PUT tools with pre-write schema validation; context-mode-index the exemplar papers to save tokens over revision cycles.
