# E031 closed and E032 repeat-recovery path frozen

## Stance

`/work` → `/interpret` → `/scout` → `/plan`

## What changed

E031 completed its frozen Tuckute consensus-transfer and LeBel repeat-information gates.
The independently audited classifications are `NO CONSENSUS INCREMENT` and `BUILDER INSTRUMENT FLOOR`.
The consensus target retained modest fixed-cohort information, but its `+0.000936` improvement over uniform residual averaging missed the `+0.002` allocation threshold.
The whole-cortex LeBel builder instrument reached only \(R_{\mathrm{builder}}=0.000116\), below the `0.01` floor, so the independent evaluation repeats stayed sealed.

Those results close the two exact E031 implementations.
They do not show that denoising, localized fMRI response recovery, or biological supervision generally fail.
The strongest recorded alternative for C2 is spatial dilution plus cortex-wide over-shrinkage, but that post-result explanation cannot license a modified E031 analysis.

## Durable next direction

The next prospective test moves to repeated time-resolved measurement rather than tuning the failed fMRI instrument.
E032 freezes a single-participant, single-story Kymata assay:

- one common shrinkage-regularized CorrCA spatial filter;
- builder runs 1 and 3, with cross-exposure folds;
- sealed evaluation only across runs 2 and 4;
- 204 MEG gradiometers primary and 64-channel EEG secondary;
- one-component PCA, shifted-filter, and shifted-template controls; and
- explicit builder, cross-run reliability, method-increment, artifact, scale, and block-bootstrap gates.

Koskinen and Seppä's SimCCA precedent was corrected before lock: it also uses one shared filter across repeats.
CorrCA remains primary because its covariance objective, shrinkage choice, component count, and held-out evaluation are easier to state and audit under Kymata's small repeat budget.

## Acquisition boundary

The live Kymata archive is not endpoint-ready from metadata alone.
Participant E2 is the lowest metadata-complete English candidate, but the event tables conflict with declared run durations unless a raw sample offset resolves them.
The paper and live archive also disagree about English acquisition hardware and calibration, the paper says 70 EEG electrodes while the sidecars expose 64, and the paper's CC BY 4.0 license conflicts with the archive's CC0 metadata.

E032 therefore starts with a raw-header, `STI101`, complete-window, channel, and calibration integrity transaction.
No Kymata participant neural sample has been opened.
The frozen config remains unauthorized until Erfan explicitly issues `/work E032`.

## Boundary

E032 can establish only fixed-participant, fixed-story cross-recording-block repeat stability beyond its locked nuisance model.
Even a pass licenses only a later content-specific assay.
It does not license a biological target, a language-model loss, layer selection, a gradient claim, student training, or participant-population inference.

Erfan's confirmation of E031's scoped classifications remains pending before any manuscript synchronization.
