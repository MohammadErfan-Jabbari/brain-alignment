\subsection{Consequential corrections}

Nine corrections govern how earlier records may be used.
First, E003 supports quality-aware headroom whose source remains unresolved, not a claim that \ac{kd} uniquely destroys or preserves alignment. \evd{E003}
Second, E004 and E005 seed--fold cells share evaluation data: fold-unit inference supersedes the 15-cell bootstrap, and E008 supplies the participant-unit test. \evd{E004}\evd{E005}\evd{E008}
Third, E006 voxel-bootstrap intervals and the derived E007 power claim are retracted; the retained statement is conditional on UTS03 and five held-out story blocks. \evd{E006}
Fourth, E010b random subsets supersede E010's nested monotone dose-response reading; E014 instead establishes the defensible signal-to-noise and estimand effect of averaging. \evd{E010}\evd{E014}
Fifth, E015 replaces the initial narrow-family, best-layer correlation with a tokenizer-independent 22-model, six-family mid-layer analysis; full-range coupling survives, while the capable-band relation remains uncertain. \evd{E015}
Sixth, E021's phase-randomized control supersedes the earlier cognition-specific reading. \evd{E021}
Seventh, the Tuckute value $0.491$ is a correlation ceiling and cannot divide variance-scale unique $R^2$; the raw contrasts and verdicts are unchanged. \evd{E002}
Eighth, E016's block permutation is defective and \texttt{textfeat} is dimension-matched only; E025 supplies the clean near-zero direct participant contrast, and E026 shows that the relative comparator cannot support attribution to neural target content. \evd{E016}\evd{E025}\evd{E026}
Ninth, E030 localizes failure at exact-target practical linear measurability, while the aligned-minus-twin contrast remains unresolved; it does not establish why E025 failed. \evd{E030}
