# Experiment — E015: the alignment ∝ −perplexity LAW across model families (matched-ppl control, generalized)

**Created:** 2026-06-12 · **Status:** COMPLETE · **Mode:** working (analysis-support)
**Question:** Does brain-alignment track LM perplexity *across* architectures/families (gpt2 / pythia / Qwen), or only *within* the gpt2 lineage (E003's r=−0.88)? If cross-family, then any brain-tuning gain reported vs a non-perplexity-matched baseline is exposed to the L011 LM-quality confound — which is what makes matched-perplexity the load-bearing control (the paper's genuinely-novel control, per the v0.9 premortem).

## Design
8 off-the-shelf LMs, 3 families (distilgpt2, gpt2, gpt2-medium, gpt2-large; pythia-1b; Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B/3B). For each: (a) held-out perplexity on a **fair natural-text probe** (WikiText-103 test sentences), (b) brain-alignment = unique R² (mid-layer `n//2`, beyond length/position/static nuisance) on the standard participant-averaged Tuckute target. Fit unique-R² vs log-ppl across models (Pearson r + Fisher CI + OLS). `scripts/run_ppl_alignment_law.py` → `outputs/E015_ppl_alignment_law.json`.

## The methodological catch (counter-argument, fable — caught BEFORE recording a wrong conclusion)
**v1 measured perplexity on the 1000 Tuckute STIMULUS sentences** (short, designed "baseline-set" text). That probe **misranked capability**: it rated Qwen2.5-3B (18T tokens, 2024) at ppl 106 — *worse* than gpt2-large (40B tokens, 2019) at 72 — a clear quality inversion (a 151k-vocab model spreads softmax mass over a larger support on atypical English). The token-count check ruled out a tokenizer fix (Qwen 7847 vs gpt2 7864 tokens). v1 cross-family r was a misleading **−0.235**, and I (wrongly) read it as "the coupling is within-lineage only." The counter-argument predicted that a fair x-axis would push r *more negative*. **It was exactly right.**

**Pearson r(log-ppl, unique R²) = −0.929, 95% CI [−0.987, −0.650] (n=8, 3 families).** OLS slope −0.017 per nat of log-ppl. The fair probe also fixes the capability ordering (Qwen2.5-3B best ppl, distilgpt2 worst). Alignment is monotone in (approx) training-data scale: distilgpt2 < gpt2 < pythia < Qwen.

**What it licenses (scoped honestly):**
- **Cross-family, brain-alignment on the standard averaged benchmark is largely a function of LM quality (perplexity)** — generalizing E003's within-gpt2 r=−0.88 to architectures/families/scales. So a brain-tuning study reporting an alignment (or downstream) gain vs a *non-perplexity-matched* baseline can be reporting an LM-quality effect, not a brain-specific one → **matched-perplexity is the necessary control** (L011 generalized; the paper's novel control, now shown to bite cross-family).
- **Caveats (counter-argument):** (1) n=8 = 3 family clusters; the CI excludes 0 but the cross-family signal rides the capability/data axis (Qwen high-data aligns higher AND lower-ppl than gpt2). (2) Within-family r's are flat/noisy (gpt2 −0.58, Qwen +0.04, n=3 each, saturated narrow ppl range) — the law is a *capability-range* law, not a within-saturated-family one. (3) y-axis uses the geometric middle layer; a per-model best-layer sweep is the refinement (not expected to weaken r given it's already −0.93). (4) The "Qwen aligns better" gap is most parsimoniously training-data scale, not architecture.

## Best-layer robustness check (counter-argument caveat #6 — CLOSED)
Re-ran with each model at its **peak-alignment layer** over a depth grid {0.4,0.5,0.6,0.7,0.8}·nlayers (not the geometric middle): cross-family **r = −0.917, 95% CI [−0.985, −0.600]** — essentially identical to the middle-layer −0.929 (`outputs/E015_ppl_alignment_law_bestlayer.json`). Peak layers shifted (e.g. Qwen2.5-3B best at L25 not L18; alignment values rose slightly) but **the cross-family law is invariant to the layer choice.** Importantly, the **within-family r's flip sign** with layer choice (gpt2 +0.38 best-layer vs −0.58 middle; Qwen −0.20 vs +0.04) — confirming the within-family correlations are noise (n=3, saturated narrow ppl range); **only the cross-family/capability law is robust.** So claim ONLY the cross-family law, never a within-family one.

## Novelty (lit-scout grounded, 2026-06-12 — do NOT overclaim)
The raw finding (alignment tracks LM quality/perplexity) is **established, not novel**: Schrimpf 2021 (43 models, brain-predictivity ∝ next-word-prediction r=0.44), Hong 2024 (4 families, log-linear ppl↓→encoding↑), [Antonello & Huth 2023](../literature/canonical/antonello-2023_scaling-laws-fmri-encoding.md) (scaling law, **r=0.91 within OPT text family** 125M–30B, +4.4%/order-of-magnitude; LLaMA ~5%>OPT at matched size, ppl-uncontrolled — note: r=0.991 is their *Whisper audio* family, NOT OPT; corrected per the canonical digest), Caucheteux & King 2022, [Gao 2024](../literature/canonical/gao-2024_scaling-not-instruction-brain-alignment.md) (alignment↑ with scale). E015's r≈−0.92 is a *tighter cross-modern-family + unique-R²* characterization, not a discovery. **The genuinely novel step is normative:** no brain-tuning paper (Schwartz/Aw/Moussa/Negi/Bilgin) perplexity-matches its baseline or raises the LM-quality-confound; E015 turns the established correlation into a *required control* for brain-tuning claims. (Caveat: [Aw & Toneva 2023](../literature/canonical/aw-2023_narrative-summarization-improves-brain-alignment.md) showed gain-WITHOUT-better-ppl — the inverse direction; our control tests gain-BECAUSE-of-better-ppl, untested.) Pasquiou 2022: the ppl↔brain relation is non-monotone when RNN+transformer classes mix — so claim the law only within modern decoder transformers.

**Why reopen a COMPLETE experiment.** The v2 verdict's load-bearing caveat is *n=8 = only 3 family clusters; the law rides the capability/data axis.* For I1 (this expansion) and especially I2 (the matched-ppl control on an external result), that caveat must be beaten: a "matched-perplexity is the required control" argument is only as strong as the cross-family law it rests on. Roadmap item I1.

**Two questions, not one (the second is the scientifically new, I2-relevant one):**
- **Q1 — generalization (robustness):** Does alignment∝−log-ppl hold across **≥6 families** (add **OPT**, **Llama-3.2**, **Mistral**, plus full **pythia** + **OPT** scaling ladders), and survive **leave-one-family-out** (drop each family, recompute r) and **rank** (Spearman) statistics? If r stays strongly negative and no single family's removal collapses it, the "rides one cluster" fear is dead.
- **Q2 — residual-after-ppl (architecture beyond quality):** After regressing out log-ppl, is there **residual alignment variance explained by family/architecture**? Antonello & Huth 2023 found *LLaMA > OPT at matched params*. If LLaMA/Mistral align higher than OPT/gpt2 **at matched perplexity**, then perplexity is *necessary but not sufficient* → the matched-ppl control is *incomplete* and architecture/data-mix is a second axis (a refinement, publishable). If the residual ≈ 0, then cross-family alignment **is** a function of ppl → matched-ppl is the *complete* control (the cleaner story for I2). **Either outcome informs I2; neither is assumed.**

**Models (~21, ≥6 families; all one consistent code path, no mixing with v2's recorded numbers):**

**Method (identical to v2 except the model set + analysis; comparability preserved):**

A near-zero residual is reported as "matched-ppl is the complete cross-family control" (the I2-favorable result). No rung flips; this is analysis-support, not a ladder rung.

## I1 DESIGN — REVISED per oracle-reviewer HOLD→PASS (fable, 2026-06-12)
The pre-compute gate returned **HOLD** with one fatal flaw and three structural ones; all adopted (this is the design that runs):
1. **[FATAL F1] x-axis = bits-per-byte, NOT per-token perplexity.** Per-token NLL is not comparable across tokenizers — vocab spans ~32k (Mistral) → 50k (gpt2/OPT/pythia) → 128k (Llama-3.2) → 151k (Qwen), and **vocab size correlates with family**, so per-token ppl injects a family-correlated nuisance straight into x. That would make a Q2 "architecture residual" potentially just a vocab-granularity artifact. Fix: **bits-per-byte** (total NLL in bits ÷ total UTF-8 bytes of the probe) is the canonical tokenizer-independent LM metric (Pile/GPT-3). **bpb is PRIMARY**; per-token ppl kept only for continuity with v2. L030 was only *partly* closed by the natural-text probe — per-token normalization is the rest of it.
2. **[F2] Tokenization standardization.** BOS differs by family (Llama/Mistral prepend BOS; gpt2/pythia none; OPT a leading `</s>`). For bpb scoring: **prepend one leading token (bos else eos) uniformly** and score all content tokens; **raise the probe `max_length` to 160** so the ≤60-word sentences never truncate (truncation = uneven text per tokenizer); force **`padding_side="right"`**; emit a per-model diagnostic table `(vocab, adds_bos, n_tokens_scored, n_bytes)`. Alignment extraction uses **`add_special_tokens=False`** uniformly (no-op for the original 8, which add none → reproduction preserved; removes BOS for new families → clean cross-family y).
3. **[F3] Pseudo-replication — scaling ladders are NOT independent draws.** 8→21 narrows the Fisher CI partly mechanically; pythia/OPT checkpoints move along one scale parameter (the L015 sin in model-space). Fix: **primary robustness = leave-one-family-out + within-family scaling slopes** (independent direction replications); **family-CLUSTER bootstrap** (resample families, not models) for the headline interval; report effective n ≈ #(family×ppl-regime) cells; Spearman over 21 is near-tautological → reported, not headlined.
4. **[confounds]** **Primary y = MIDDLE layer (0.5)** (best-layer is a post-hoc max → forking-path; demote to robustness); kill criteria stated on middle-layer. **Q2 averaging guard (L016):** re-fit the law on **individual-subject targets** (UIDs 848/853/797 at mid-layer) — confirm the cross-family sign holds per-subject, else a Q2 residual could be architecture predicting the shared-stimulus component, not brain alignment. **ANCOVA common-support precondition:** families occupy near-disjoint ppl bands (Lord's-paradox / collinearity) → if no x-overlap, Q2 can only **bound** the family effect; **n<3 families (Mistral n=1, Llama n=2) excluded from the family factor** (descriptive only). fp32 disclosed (absolute bpb won't match published bf16 numbers).
**Internal-consistency gate (oracle's KILL condition):** the original 8 models, re-run on bpb, must reproduce the v2 cross-family law (sign + strength). If bpb breaks it, the v2 −0.92 was a per-token-tokenizer artifact and the matched-ppl backbone needs rebuilding before expanding. **Verdict gate = PASS** with the above folded in.
