# Plain perplexity-only KD does not preserve a teacher's brain alignment for free, but at this screen the loss cannot be separated from language-model quality

## The verdict

Plain perplexity-only KD does not preserve a teacher's brain alignment by default: the Q1 kill-test does not return preserve-for-free. The verdict arm, a from-scratch logit-KD student, lands far below gpt2-medium on the Tuckute ROI screen, so we rule out the outcome that would have made the thesis's distillation question a non-problem. That verdict rests on the cold-init arm, the one arm that isolates the KD channel: the only converged pure-perplexity-only arm does not by itself fall below the teacher, so the claim is that plain KD does not preserve alignment *for free*, not that the objective sheds it. The screen is coarse by design (five LH language ROIs, NC $\approx 0.35$) and the arms are matched on budget, not perplexity, so this is a statement about preserve-versus-shed at that screen, not a resolution of the $\approx 0.005$ objective-specific gap [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md). R07 reuses R06's apparatus unchanged and adds only the KD contrast and a statistic that sizes the gap; it answers Q1, the gate one rung above R06's "the signal is real beyond confounds."

## The question, and why it is empirical

Once R06 fixed that the alignment signal is real, the first question is whether an aggressive but capability-preserving transform keeps it, and for KD that is an empirical question rather than a corollary of the compression bound. The data-processing inequality bounds a fixed-function compression (quantization or pruning), where the student depends on the stimulus only through the teacher's features, so $A_S \le A_T$ holds there; but a from-scratch KD student has a stimulus side-channel through the raw next-token targets that bypasses those features, the Markov premise fails, and DPI does not bound it, so whether KD preserves alignment must be measured. The DPI ceiling stands only as the fixed-function regime the KD result is read against; R07 measures the gap directly rather than leaning on DPI to predict it, and indeed the table shows conventional gpt2 scoring at or above gpt2-medium, which a hard bound would forbid. The headroom $\Delta = A_T - A_S$ is therefore the quantity of interest: this report measures the size of the shed, not whether an alignment-guided objective can recover it.

## The design and the statistic

Cold-init is the verdict arm because a warm-started student begins at full teacher-grade alignment (R06's E002 puts pretrained gpt2 at $\approx +0.020$), so its retention would measure fine-tuning drift rather than what the distillation channel transmits; we keep warm-init KD as a labelled drift control. The cold-init arm is the one place where any alignment the student ends with was transmitted through the KD channel, not inherited from pretraining, which is what the DPI side-channel argument is about. The rationale rests on a single E002 warm-start measurement standing in for "warm-init begins at teacher-grade alignment," an unreplicated point at the same coarse screen rather than a swept curve. The verdict statistic is floor-anchored retention $\rho'$, the fraction of the teacher's alignment above the untrained floor that the student retains, with $\rho'=0$ meaning fell back to the floor and $\rho'=1$ meaning kept the teacher's alignment; we read the verdict on $\rho'$ with its bootstrap CI together with $\Delta$ and a paired test, never on a point ratio. We hold the verdict comparison entirely within 12-layer GPT-2 arms at a fixed layer (L7), so there is no cross-architecture layer confound and no winner's-curse layer selection [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md).

## The evidence

The cold-init from-scratch logit-KD verdict arm lands far below the teacher: $\Delta = +0.018$, $p < 0.001$, floor-anchored retention $\rho' = 0.37$ [0.14, 0.58] [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md). Table 1 gives the full verdict-layer table per arm: NC-normalised unique $R^2$, floor-anchored retention $\rho'$ with its bootstrap 95% CI, $\Delta$ versus the teacher, the bootstrap probability of no drop, and held-out perplexity.

| Arm | init / objective | unique $R^2$ | $\rho'$ [bootstrap CI] | $\Delta$ vs teacher | $p$(no-drop) | ppl |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| teacher gpt2-medium | pretrained | $+0.0188$ | n/a | n/a | n/a | 74 [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) |
| gpt2 | pretrained (conventional) | $+0.0195$ | 1.02 [0.77, 1.27] | $\approx 0$ | 0.57 | 105 [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) |
| lmft_warm | warm + plain LM finetune | $+0.0174$ | 0.95 [0.81, 1.13] | $+0.0016$ | 0.265 | 44 [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) |
| kd_warm | warm + pure logit KD | $+0.0144$ | 0.84 [0.67, 1.04] | $+0.0046$ | 0.061 | 95 [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) |
| distilgpt2 | real distillation (6L/82M, +hidden-cosine) | $+0.0076$ | 0.60 [0.42, 0.84] | $+0.0119$ | 0.001 | 169 [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) |
| **kd_cold** | **random + pure logit KD** | $+0.0005$ | **0.37 [0.14, 0.58]** | $+0.0181$ | 0.000 | 477 [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) |
| untrained | random (floor) | $-0.0102$ | 0 | n/a | n/a | $\approx 6\times10^4$ [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) |

**Table 1.** Per-arm brain alignment on the Tuckute ROI screen at the verdict layer (L7). The distilgpt2 $\rho'$ is anchored to its own gpt2 teacher, not gpt2-medium [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md).

Two cautions bound that verdict. First, the arm is under-trained: its held-out perplexity is 477 against the converged arms' 44–95, so part of its near-floor alignment reflects a worse language model rather than distillation-specific shedding, and it ran 2 epochs to the warm arms' 1, so even the step count was not matched; this is the predeclared convergence guard, not a post-hoc excuse. Second, the drop is carried by this cold arm because it is the one arm that isolates the KD channel: the only converged pure-perplexity-only arm, kd_warm, does not by itself reject preservation ($\Delta = +0.0046$, $p = 0.061$, bootstrap CI [$-0.0011$, $+0.0103$] including 0), so "does not preserve for free" rests on the cold arm, on a point estimate that is itself rank-fragile (below), rather than on the clean converged arm [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md).

The durable finding is a monotone alignment gradient ordered by how much of each model's representation was rebuilt through the KD channel rather than inherited from pretraining: conventional gpt2 ($\rho' \approx 1.0$) $>$ warm-init KD (0.84) $>$ distilgpt2 (0.60) $>$ cold-init KD (0.37) $>$ the untrained floor (0); the ordering is stable across PCA rank [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md). Re-computing $\rho'$ per arm at $n_{\text{pca}} = 25/50/100$ holds the ordering at every rank. The ordering is descriptive, not causal: the same ranking is equally explained by language-model quality, which is collinear with rebuild-extent across the arms. The no-teacher control makes that collinearity concrete: lmft_warm rebuilds less of its representation than warm-init KD yet aligns higher ($\rho' = 0.95$ vs $0.84$), in step with its lower perplexity (44 vs 95), so what is durable is the ordering itself, while rebuild-extent is a descriptive label collinear with language-model quality, not an independent axis.

Only the gradient's ordering is durable; the absolute unique-$R^2$ magnitudes are PCA-rank-sensitive, and the two confounded arms (cold-init KD, distilgpt2) sign-flip across the rank sweep: cold-init KD reads $+0.009 / -0.004 / -0.010$ and distilgpt2 $+0.017 / +0.008 / -0.004$ at $n_{\text{pca}} = 25/50/100$, while the ordering is preserved at every rank [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md). The durable claim is therefore restricted to the ordering, and the quoted shed fractions should be read as rank-dependent, not exact.

This screen does not establish the stronger claim that the distillation objective sheds alignment beyond the language-model quality it costs: alignment co-varies with log-perplexity at Pearson $r = -0.88$, the two objective-specific dissociations are only marginal ($P(\text{gpt2} \le \text{kd\_warm}) = 0.092$ and $P(\text{lmft} \le \text{kd\_warm}) = 0.085$, both $p \approx 0.09$), and the verdict arm is under-trained, so the objective's contribution is undemonstrated here rather than shown to be zero [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md) [L011](../learnings.md). Plotting alignment against held-out log-perplexity exposes that relation directly: a tight negative scatter of the five trained and distilled arms, fitted line $\text{align} = 0.050 - 0.0079\,\ln(\text{ppl})$, with conventional gpt2 the lone positive outlier. The non-result is itself power- and construction-limited: the two $P$-values flip to $\approx 0.00$ under a fold-only bootstrap, lean on a single unreplicated gpt2 point and one fold carrying $\approx 44\%$ of the signal, and the coarse screen is underpowered for a $\approx 0.005$ gap. The within-arm coupling itself stands only as a hedge against the strong claim, not a standalone result: its bootstrap CI is [$-1.0$, $+0.31$], and E015 later showed the within-family alignment-perplexity correlation to be layer-unstable noise, keeping only a layer-stable cross-family quality law [E003](../experiments/E003_kd-alignment-preservation.md).

## What it means

R07 sizes the headroom; it does not adjudicate the objective. The thesis-deciding form of the question is at matched perplexity on a powered voxelwise substrate (R06's E006 apparatus), not matched budget on this ROI screen, and that resolution moves to Q2 and Q3. So Q1 is neither a non-problem (plain KD plainly does not preserve for free) nor a confirmation that an alignment-guided objective has a job; the matched-perplexity, OOD comparison that decides that runs in the later reports, not pre-judged here. The discipline R06 fixed carries forward: real is not the same as shed-by-objective, and this report establishes only that plain KD sheds, not why. That is why the size of the shed, not its existence, is the quantity worth carrying forward: Q1 is the gate to the program, not its main result.
