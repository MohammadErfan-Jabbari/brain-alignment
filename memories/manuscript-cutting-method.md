# Manuscript Cutting Method

Once a section conveys its message, Erfan wants an explicit cut pass, "like Elon Musk", and he reframed the question tree itself as a requirement that the cut may change: "the cutting stage can also mean updating the tree, as we might be able to combine paragraphs. don't be afraid of that. make the decision that is necessary, not blindly following a bunch of rules."

**The method, in order.** (1) Question the requirement: does the reader need this *claim* at all? (2) Delete the part. (3) Only then simplify the wording. The failure mode is skipping to step 3 and polishing a sentence that should not exist.

**What the Section 2 pass taught (2026-09-01, 2,393 to 1,895 words, all 22 citations kept):**
- A merged or struck leaf outranks any sentence-level cut of the same size, because it deletes the *reason* the words existed. Two 2.4 leaves were struck or narrowed and the prose followed.
- Every deletion needs a re-read of what pointed at it. Deleted sentences carried a bridge (permutation evidence to the text-derived requirement), a term's first construction ("permuted" then appeared six times before its definition), an antecedent for "This", and once the paragraph's actual claim.
- Some passages need *more* words. Two independent methods, allocate-for-compounding and remove-bottlenecks, converged on 2.2 as the binding constraint and as under-written: it named target uptake and retained-student movement without saying they *are* the manipulation claim. Three added words fixed it.
- A downstream section re-glossing a term parenthetically is direct evidence the definition upstream is under-written. Look for that symptom before cutting a definition.
- Reject a cut whose only justification is word count when the sentence is the sole declaration of a rule the reader has no other source for, even if a later section applies the rule.
- Erfan overruled my recommended section title and edited the tree instead. Committee-conventional names win over tree-accurate ones; the tree follows him, not the reverse.
- Verify citation survival mechanically after any cut to a related-work section. Check the page count honestly: 132 words saved reflowed into zero pages.

**What Sections 4 and 5 added (2026-09-01/02; 22.1% and 20.5%).**
- Erfan's diagnosis of the Sections 1-3 shortfall, in his words: "I expected at least 20 percent of section 1 and section 2 and section 3 to be cut." Four causes: no declared word budget, so "shorter" had no target; narrowing leaves instead of striking them, which preserves the reason the words exist; moving text instead of deleting it; and the review round restoring what the cut round removed. A declared per-subsection budget fixed it immediately.
- State three rules in the briefing or agents will violate all three: **narrowing is not cutting**, **moving is not deleting** (a relocation counts zero words unless the receiving section already states it), and **question the requirement before the wording**.
- **The deepest cuts are re-derivation, not wording.** Section 5 lost 85 words in one stroke because `07:19` was verbatim identical to `05:56` and `02:167` stated `05:87` word for word. Grep a candidate sentence against the whole tree before polishing it.
- **The honest number is the post-verification number.** Section 5 read 24.3% before verification and 20.5% after, because the extra points were a claim the record denied and a leaf gap the cut had opened. Report the second number.
- Two passages needed *more* words in Section 5, both for the same reason: a recommendation is unreadable without the observed failure it repairs. Saying the assay ran *after* training, when nothing could redirect it, turned a generic recipe into a lesson.

**Why:** the cuts that mattered were requirement deletions, not wording; and the most valuable finding of the pass added words. A word-count target alone would have produced neither.

**How to apply:** run the cut pass only after the review pass has fixed correctness. Use fresh agents with three distinct methods and treat convergence as the signal (see [Subagent Orchestration for Manuscript Review](subagent-orchestration-for-manuscript-review.md)). Before applying any cut, name what depends on the deleted sentence. Extends [Manuscript Revision Philosophy](manuscript-revision-philosophy.md).
