# No Walls of Text

When working a task, not just in final answers, never emit a dense paragraph block of status or reasoning. Erfan flagged a multi-line wall-of-text mid-task message as unreadable.

**Why:** he scans; a wall of prose forces linear reading and he will not do it. He would rather see nothing during the work than a block he has to wade through.

**How to apply:** during a task, either do the work silently with minimal narration, or, if reporting is useful, lead with the one-line point, then whitespace, short lines, or a small table. No dense multi-sentence paragraphs as progress updates.
