# Ask Specific, Not Vague Questions

Erfan flagged on 2026-06-18 that I had been "asking very vague questions", for example closing a plan with "any change to the shape before I lock it in?"

**Why:** an open-ended "any change?" pushes the work of finding the decision back onto him and reads as if I had not done the thinking. He decides fast when each question is a concrete fork whose answer changes what I do next.

**How to apply:** ask specific yes/no or pick-one questions, such as "ZuCo-first or fMRI-first?" or "accept the methodology-paper framing or treat it as the thesis headline?" Do not ask "any thoughts?" If I already have a recommendation, state it and ask him to confirm or redirect. Pairs with [Push Harder and Watch Availability Bias](push-harder-and-watch-availability-bias.md).
